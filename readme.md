# Race all of the colors of the rainbow in Rainbow Racers, a 3D "rainbows and unicorn" themed racing game.

Rainbow Racers features two game modes, The Rainbow Cup, and Instant Race.

In the Rainbow Cup, you compete with other unicorn racers for the coveted rainbow cup over seven unique race tracks.

In Instant Race, you select your track options, click the generate button until you get a nice track, and then race!

### HOW TO PLAY:

Choose your game mode, then control your unicorn racer using the `W` `A` `S` and `D` keys. What could be simpler?

### TIPS:

1. Hug the curves to gain on your opponents.
2. Run over booster pads to get a temporary speed buff.
3. Bunting opponents will boost them, but being bunted by an opponent will boost you!

### FEATURES:

- Full HD graphics.
- Colorful 3D game world.
- Multiple game modes.
- Configurable persistent controls and audio settings.
- Persistent best lap times.
- 3D engine sound, try it with headphones!
- Runs fast on potato computers.
- It actually has Rainbows and Unicorns!
- A complete game, there are no fixed controls, and it has a campaign with an ending.

### NOTES:

Rainbow Racers has been tested working in Firefox and Chrome web browsers on Windows 11. Your mileage may vary with other browsers.
